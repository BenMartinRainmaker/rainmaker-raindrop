# Market note
The private market has split into two distinct tiers! At the top, the hottest tech companies command the strongest demand. Anthropic's employees declined to sell shares at the $350B tender price in April, and within weeks, secondary desks were pricing the company north of $1 trillion. Now, with the IPO in focus, price talk is in the $1.3T+ range. OpenAI's $7B August tender cleared flat at $852B, while its rival now trades above it on the secondary tape. Databricks is changing hands at or above its $190B round while its IPO drifts to 2027. Beyond these headliners, the picture looks markedly different. Trading volume on Rainmaker jumped 57% quarter over quarter in Q3, yet roughly 84% of trades cleared below the last primary valuation. Liquidity is abundant, but it's only flowing into a handful of names, and sellers elsewhere are accepting discounts to complete trades. With the IPO window stuck half-open, secondaries aren't the side door anymore. They have become the main route to liquidity, and buyer access is limited.

# Introduction
Against this backdrop, Rainmaker Securities presents Anduril as the November 2026 Raindrop of the Month! Founded in 2017, Anduril has rapidly established itself at the forefront of modern homeland defense. Rather than sending thousands of foot soldiers into enemy territory, Anduril builds battle-ready machines and sensors designed to be controlled from overseas. From AI-piloted vehicles to its state-of-the-art device Lattice, an AI command system coordinating and directing machines simultaneously, Anduril has revolutionized the frontier of warfare. In less than a decade, it has cemented itself among the most closely watched private companies and one of the most requested names on our desk.

# What they build
## Autonomous aircraft (aircraft)
- Fury: AI wingman jet for F-35s.
- Ghost: Army reconnaissance drone.
- Altius, Bolt-M: tube and backpack strike drones.
- Omen: long-range VTOL drone, new in 2026.
## Missiles & interceptors (missile)
- Barracuda: mass-produced cruise missile.
- Roadrunner: reusable jet interceptor.
- Rocket motors: 6,000 a year in Mississippi.
## Sensors & scanners (sensor)
- Sentry towers: border and base security.
- Wisp: infrared camera that spots threats itself.
- Seabed Sentry: ocean-floor sensor nodes.
## Air & space defense (shield)
- Anvil: rams hostile drones out of the sky.
- Pulsar: jammer in U.S. service.
- Golden Dome: space interceptor prototypes.
- Anduril-26: first satellite, launched July 2026.
## Underwater vehicles (underwater)
- Ghost Shark: extra-large sub, U.S. and Australia.
- Dive-LD: long-range sub in Navy service.
- Copperhead: torpedo drone fired from subs.
- Surface vessels: unmanned ships with HD Hyundai.
## Soldier & command systems (soldier)
- EagleEye: mixed-reality helmet from Microsoft.
- Menace: command center in a container.

# Feature band
title: Lattice: AI central command
Every Anduril system reports into Lattice: a singular AI command and control platform (the AI general of the army). Lattice processes all these feeds at once, calculates, and directs thousands of machines simultaneously to do its bidding in real time.
