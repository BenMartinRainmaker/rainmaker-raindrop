The private market has split into two! At the top, Anthropic's employees passed on selling at the $350B tender price in April, and within weeks, secondary desks were pricing the company above $1 trillion. The IPO filing is now in, and price talk is above $1.5T. OpenAI's $7B August tender cleared at $852B, flat to its last round, and Anthropic now trades above OpenAI on the secondary market. Databricks is changing hands at or above its $190B round while its IPO slips to 2027. Below those names, the picture is different. Third-quarter volume on Rainmaker was up 57% from the second quarter, but roughly 84% of trades cleared below the company's last primary valuation. There is plenty of liquidity, but most of it is going to a short list of companies, and sellers elsewhere are accepting discounts to get trades done. With the IPO window only partly open, secondaries have become the main route to liquidity, and buyers are choosing carefully.

Amongst all the mayhem, Rainmaker Securities presents Anduril as the November 2026 Raindrop of the Month! Founded in 2017, Anduril has rapidly established itself at the forefront of modern homeland defense. Instead of sending thousands of foot soldiers into enemy territory, Anduril builds battle-ready machines and sensors designed to be controlled from overseas. From AI-piloted vehicles and state-of-the-art devices to Lattice (an AI command system coordinating and directing all the machines at once), Anduril has revolutionized the frontier of warfare. Just nine years in, they are one of the most requested names on our desk.

What they build

Autonomous aircraft
  • Fury: AI wingman jet for F-35s.
  • Ghost: Army reconnaissance drone.
  • Altius, Bolt-M: tube and backpack strike drones.
  • Omen: long-range VTOL drone, new in 2026.
Missiles & interceptors
  • Barracuda: mass-produced cruise missile.
  • Roadrunner: reusable jet interceptor.
  • Rocket motors: 6,000 a year in Mississippi.
Sensors & scanners
  • Sentry towers: border and base security.
  • Wisp: infrared camera that spots threats itself.
  • Seabed Sentry: ocean-floor sensor nodes.
Air & space defense
  • Anvil: rams hostile drones out of the sky.
  • Pulsar: jammer in U.S. service.
  • Golden Dome: space interceptor prototypes.
  • Anduril-26: first satellite, launched July 2026.
Underwater vehicles
  • Ghost Shark: extra-large sub, U.S. and Australia.
  • Dive-LD: long-range sub in Navy service.
  • Copperhead: torpedo drone fired from subs.
  • Surface vessels: unmanned ships with HD Hyundai.
Soldier & command systems
  • EagleEye: mixed-reality helmet from Microsoft.
  • Menace: command center in a container.

Customers

  • U.S. Army: 10-year Lattice contract, $20B ceiling.
  • U.S. Air Force: Fury for the CCA fleet.
  • U.S. Navy: sub components, Dive-LD, and Roadrunner.
  • U.S. Marines: 600+ Bolt-M strike drones.
  • Border Protection: 500+ sentry towers ordered.
  • Allies: Australia, Taiwan, UAE, Poland, and Kuwait.
Lattice: AI central command
Every Anduril system reports into Lattice: a singular AI command and control platform (the AI general of the army). Lattice processes all these feeds at once, calculates, and directs thousands of machines simultaneously to do its bidding in real time.
