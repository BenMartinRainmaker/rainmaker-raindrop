Instead of sending thousands of foot soldiers into enemy territory, Anduril builds battle-ready machines and sensors designed to be controlled from overseas. From AI-piloted vehicles to state-of-the-art devices to Lattice (an AI command system coordinating and directing all the machines at once), Anduril has revolutionized the frontier of warfare! Just nine years in, they are one of the most requested names on our desk.

Customers

  • U.S. Army: 10-year Lattice contract, $20B ceiling.
  • U.S. Air Force: Fury production for the CCA fleet.
  • U.S. Navy: Virginia-class sub parts, Dive-LD, Roadrunner.
  • U.S. Marines: 600+ Bolt-M strike drones.
  • Border Protection: 300+ sentry towers, 200 more ordered.
  • Australia: Ghost Shark subs, A$1.7B program.
  • Taiwan: 2,000+ Altius drones, deliveries from Q4 2026.
  • UAE, Poland, Kuwait: Omen, Barracuda, counter-drone kits.

What they build

Autonomous aircraft
  • Fury: AI-piloted wingman jet for F-35s.
  • Ghost: reconnaissance drone used by the U.S. Army.
  • Altius: tube-launched strike drone.
  • Bolt-M: backpack strike drone for the Marines.
  • Omen: long-range VTOL drone unveiled in 2026.
Missiles & interceptors
  • Barracuda: cruise missile built for mass production.
  • Roadrunner: reusable jet-powered interceptor.
  • Rocket motors: 6,000 a year from its Mississippi plant.
Sensors & scanners
  • Sentry towers: autonomous border and base towers.
  • Wisp: infrared camera that spots threats on its own.
  • Seabed Sentry: sensor nodes on the ocean floor.
Air & space defense
  • Anvil: drone that rams hostile drones out of the sky.
  • Pulsar: jammer, in service with U.S. forces.
  • Golden Dome: space-based interceptor prototypes.
  • Anduril-26: first satellite, launched July 2026.
Underwater vehicles
  • Ghost Shark: extra-large sub for the U.S. and Australia.
  • Dive-LD: long-range sub in U.S. Navy service.
  • Copperhead: torpedo drone fired from autonomous subs.
  • Surface vessels: unmanned ships built with HD Hyundai.
Soldier & command systems
  • EagleEye: mixed-reality helmet, taken over from Microsoft.
  • Menace: command center that ships in a container.
Lattice: AI central command
Every Anduril system reports into Lattice: a singular AI command and control platform (the AI general of the army). Lattice processes all these feeds at once, calculates, and directs thousands of machines simultaneously to do its bidding in real time.
